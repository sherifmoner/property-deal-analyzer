Underwriting — 5 strategies, one shell

Same math as ReiSearch. Revenue defaults come from your pulled comps: Co-Living rent/room from the ZIP room-rent benchmark, Long/Mid Term rent from the rental-comp average. Line items are JSON arrays: rehab/appliances/furnishing/conversion items use {label, qty, material, labor}; funding stack uses {type, amount, rate}. Every strategy gets a Deal Score (ReiSearch only scored Co-Living). Co-Living weights: CF/room 25% · DSCR 20% · CoC 20% · cap 15% · break-even 12% · downside CF 8%, minus realism penalties — $0 taxes/insurance get sanity defaults, never a free "Strong".
